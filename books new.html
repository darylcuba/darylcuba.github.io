ILLUSTRATION / COMIC ART / DIGITAL DRAWING

Illustrating books is both a privilege and a responsibility. For young readers especially, an illustration often becomes their first visual connection to a story. Whether interpreting literary classics by authors such as Edgar Allan Poe and Alejo Carpentier or creating artwork for contemporary publications, I strive to craft imagery that enriches the reading experience while honoring each author's unique voice.

THE WASP'S NEST

These vibrant illustrations are a selection of interior artwork created for The Wasp’s Nest, a collection featuring Agatha Christie’s legendary Hercule Poirot alongside other classic detective stories. Published by Editorial Andarele, the volume brought these iconic mysteries together and gave me the opportunity to visually reinterpret their suspense, atmosphere, and timeless characters.

THE KINGDOM

No color was left behind in bringing the magic of Alejo Carpentier’s The Kingdom of This World to life. The novel is filled with the mysticism, energy, and richness of Afro-Caribbean culture and folklore, offering endless visual inspiration. Created for an educational edition aimed at high school students, the illustrations embrace a dynamic, colorful, and slightly cartoonish visual language, making the story’s extraordinary world feel vibrant and approachable.

ALAS A LA DERIVA

Alas a la Deriva, a compelling collection of Amazonian jungle stories by Horacio Quiroga, comes to life through a series of comic-inspired scenes. For this teen-focused edition, I chose a bold, inked graphic style, using a high-contrast black-and-white palette and dynamic compositions to convey movement, drama, and visual energy throughout the illustrations.
