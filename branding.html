IDENTITY / COLLATERAL / APPLICATIONS

I've had the opportunity to create brand identities for a diverse range of clients—from bars and nightclubs to hotels, hospitals, and cultural organizations. Each project brings its own challenges, calling for a balance of strategic thinking, creativity, and thoughtful design to build identities that feel distinctive, meaningful, and made to last.

AINO

AINO is a conscious Canadian cosmeceutical brand dedicated to creating organic beauty and healthcare products with respect for the environment. Clean geometric forms and delicate shapes define the visual identity, conveying a sense of elegance, purity, and balance. A restrained color palette keeps the brand sophisticated and cohesive, while a minimalist approach to packaging reinforces its commitment to simplicity, quality, and conscious design.

MATIZ

MATIZ is a restoration and design collective founded by young Cuban visual artists, bringing an artistic perspective to the renovation, restoration, and transformation of spaces. Their approach combines the practical nature of construction with the creativity and craftsmanship of the arts. The identity was built around this intersection—where construction meets art. A bold capital “M”, subtly shaped to evoke the tip of a paintbrush, became the centerpiece of the logo, while a strong, warm color palette reinforces the brand’s balance of craftsmanship, creativity, and character.

PEQUEÑO SUAREZ

“PEQUEÑO SUAREZ”—Spanish for “Little Suarez”—is a family-owned restaurant in Varadero Beach, Cuba. With an already well-established Suarez Restaurant in town, owned by another member of the family, this new venture needed to build an identity of its own rather than be perceived as a smaller version or extension of the original. The solution was a fresh and distinctive visual identity centered around the restaurant’s initials, cleverly shaped to suggest a smoking-hot dish ready to be served. A bold, high-contrast color palette reinforces the concept with an energetic and confident personality—one that clearly says: “We’re here, we’re different, and we mean business.”
