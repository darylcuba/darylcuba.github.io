TEATRO ROSA

The 'Festival de Teatro Rosa' (Pink Theater Festival) was one of Cuba's first LGBTQ+ theater festivals, launched at a time when public support and visibility for the community remained a sensitive topic. Contributing to the visual identity of such a meaningful cultural event was both an honor and a responsibility.
I chose a bold yet approachable concept, using simple, symbolic imagery infused with humor to communicate a message of inclusion, pride, and celebration. The goal was to create an identity that was instantly recognizable, emotionally engaging, and reflective of the courage and vision of the festival's organizers and participants.




CALLEJERO IX

Over several consecutive editions, I had the opportunity to develop the visual identity and animated television campaigns for El Callejero, the International Street Theater Festival of Matanzas, Cuba. Recognized nationally and internationally, the festival attracts artists and audiences who celebrate the unique spirit of street performance.
Designing the festival's identity year after year was an especially rewarding creative challenge. Each edition required a fresh visual concept while preserving the event's recognizable character, resulting in distinctive campaigns that reflected evolving themes and audiences.
My role encompassed the complete creative direction of the festival's visual communications, including branding, posters, billboards, programs, credentials, promotional merchandise, digital assets, and the design and production of the animated TV spots.

CALLEJERO X

For the festival's 10th edition, I developed a visual identity inspired by urban and underground aesthetics, combining vibrant colors with phonetic-inspired typography to appeal to a broader, younger audience.
With an expanded program, the festival was organized into three categories — National, International, and Children's — each distinguished by its own signature color within the overall visual system. This flexible identity created a more dynamic and cohesive experience across all communication materials, including print, digital media, and the festival's TV spot.

CALLEJERO XII

The festival's post-pandemic edition was necessarily smaller in scale, yet deeply meaningful for the performers who were finally able to return to the streets and reconnect with live audiences. I once again had the opportunity to develop the event's visual identity, this time embracing a simpler aesthetic that reflected the contrast between the quiet introspection of quarantine and the renewed energy and optimism of live performance.

BEDTIME STORIES

During the pandemic, when lockdowns and social distancing reshaped everyday life, the performing arts also had to find new ways to reach their audiences. 'Teatro El Mirón Cubano', one of Cuba's most respected children's theater companies, embraced digital platforms by adapting many of its productions into online stories and social media content.
I had the opportunity to illustrate and promote this initiative by creating a series of playful animated graphics that preserved the imagination and charm of live theater while engaging young audiences in a virtual environment.
